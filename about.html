Landscape Construction for GTA Homes

LanScape Outdoors provides residential landscaping and hardscape construction across Toronto and the GTA.

Our work includes interlocking patios and driveways, retaining walls, steps, natural stone, sodding, grading, deck and fence work, and supporting site preparation.

We believe homeowners should understand what is being built — not just what the finished surface looks like. That means paying attention to excavation depth, base preparation, drainage, transitions and finishing details.
