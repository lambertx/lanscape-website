Why Construction Quality Matters

The Finished Surface Is Only Half the Job.

In the GTA, interlocking and landscape construction must deal with water, settlement and freeze-thaw cycles. The base underneath your project is just as important as the material you see.

✓
Excavation matched to the project
Driveways, patios and walkways have different load requirements.
✓
Compacted aggregate base
Proper preparation helps reduce movement and settlement.
✓
Drainage and grading considered
Water should move away from structures and usable spaces.
✓
Clean edges and transitions
The details around steps, borders and existing surfaces complete the job.
