Customer stories explain the challenge, our construction solution and the result after the project was completed.

Markham, Ontario • Backyard Interlocking

A Backyard That Grew with the Family

The Homeowner’s Challenge

As the homeowners’ children got older, the backyard needed to become more useful for everyday family life. Most of the space was lawn, which limited where the family could place outdoor furniture, barbecue, and gather comfortably after rain.

Our Recommendation

We recommended keeping part of the lawn while creating a larger interlocking patio for dining, seating, and family activities. The layout was planned to make the space feel open, easy to maintain, and practical for both adults and children.

What We Built

  • Excavation and disposal
  • Base preparation and compaction
  • Interlocking installation
  • Edge restraint and polymeric sand
  • Final grading and cleanup

The Result

The finished patio gave the family a clean outdoor living area with room for meals, entertaining, and everyday play while still preserving green space around the yard.

Richmond Hill, Ontario • Front Entrance

Making the Front Entrance Safer for the Years Ahead

The Homeowner’s Challenge

The homeowners wanted a front entrance that looked cleaner and was easier to use. The previous steps and walkway felt dated and less comfortable, especially during winter conditions.

Our Recommendation

We recommended wider, consistent steps with a durable stone finish and a smoother interlocking walkway connection to the driveway. Grading and drainage around the entrance were also considered.

The Result

The upgraded entrance created a more comfortable walking path, improved the overall appearance of the front of the home, and made the approach to the door feel more finished.

Toronto GTA • Driveway Interlocking

A New Driveway Before the “For Sale” Sign Went Up

The Homeowner’s Challenge

Before listing the property, the homeowners wanted the exterior to make a stronger first impression. The old driveway looked worn and did not match the condition of the rest of the home.

What We Built

We recommended a new interlocking driveway with a deeper vehicle-grade base, a clean border treatment, and neutral stone colours that complemented the house instead of competing with it.

The Result

The new driveway gave the property a cleaner, more updated appearance and improved curb appeal for listing photos and showings. We describe this as a presentation improvement rather than a guaranteed increase in selling price.

Real Project Story • Markham

Creating More Outdoor Living Space in a Markham Backyard

This is a real completed LanScape Outdoors project. The homeowner wanted to replace an older patio surface with a cleaner, larger outdoor area that worked better for family use and entertaining.

The Need

A more attractive and usable hardscape area for outdoor dining, seating and family activities.

The Work

New large-format interlocking, a darker border, cleaner lawn transitions and a refreshed patio layout around the existing pergola.

The Outcome

A brighter, more modern backyard with more usable space while preserving the lawn and existing outdoor structure.
